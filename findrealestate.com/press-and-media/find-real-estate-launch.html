Oxford, Spire, & Level Group Unite to Launch FIND Real Estate, Creating One of NYC’s Largest Independent Brokerages

New York, NY — Dec 1, 2025 — Three of New York City’s most established independent real estate firms — Oxford Property Group, Spire Group, and Level Group — have officially come together under one unified brand: FIND Real Estate.

 

The rebrand marks a major milestone in the NYC real estate landscape, bringing together more than 1,000 real estate agents across New York, Connecticut, New Jersey, and Florida. FIND Real Estate launches with a shared mission: to elevate the agent experience, strengthen client relationships, and modernize the way real estate is done.

 

 

A Shared Vision for a Stronger Future

FIND Real Estate was built on the belief that brokerages thrive when agents are empowered equally. Rather than one company absorbing another, the merger is the result of a mutual decision by all three firms to build something more powerful — together.

 

“FIND represents collaboration at its best,” said Adam Mahfouda, CEO and Co-Founder of FIND Real Estate. “By combining the strengths, technology, and talent of three thriving brokerages, we’re creating an environment where agents can grow faster, support their clients better, and scale their businesses with more ease than ever before.”

 

Despite the new name, FIND continues the legacy of excellence established by all three firms and remains committed to delivering industry-leading service across residential and commercial real estate.

 

For Clients, It’s Seamless

Clients will now benefit from a wider network of experienced agents, enhanced marketing capabilities, and expanded service areas — without any disruption to their experience.

 

About FIND Real Estate
FIND Real Estate is one of the largest independent real estate brokerages in New York City, with over 1,000 agents across NY, NJ, CT, and FL. Born from the union of Oxford Property Group, Spire Group, and Level Group, FIND is driven by a commitment to empower agents, modernize brokerage operations, and provide clients with exceptional real estate guidance. Through innovative tools, transparent systems, and a collaborative culture, FIND Real Estate is redefining what a modern brokerage can be.
